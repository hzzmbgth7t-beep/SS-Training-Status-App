Setup instructions

1. Open the app and sign in

Open https://hzzmbgth7t-beep.github.io/SS-Training-Status-App/ in Safari (iPhone/iPad) or Chrome (Android or computer).

Sign in with your @southernsoftware.com email and your password. No account yet? Ask Ronnie Brigman.

Forgot your password? Type your email, tap Forgot password?, and use the link in the email to set a new one.

This address always opens Training Status, even if your account also has the Conference Tracker. Administrators who have both apps see Switch App in the Menu; it opens the Conference Tracker directly.

2. Set up Face ID (or fingerprint / passkey)
  1. Sign in once with your email and password.
  2. Open the Menu and tap Set Up Face ID / Passkey.
  3. Follow your phone's prompt (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or device PIN).
  4. From then on, tap Sign in with Face ID or Passkey on the sign-in screen. No password needed.

Set it up separately on each phone or computer you use. On an iPhone, the Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, so sign in there once.

3. Add the app to your Home Screen

This gives the app its own icon and opens it full screen, without the browser's address bar.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the app address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow). On newer iPhones it is in the menu next to the address bar; on older ones it is at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If it is not in the list, tap Edit Actions and add it.
  4. Keep the name "SS Training", make sure Open as Web App is on if you see it, and tap Add.
  5. Open the new icon and sign in once (the Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in).

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the app address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add or Install to confirm.

Samsung Internet

  1. Open the app address, tap the menu (three lines) at the bottom right.
  2. Tap Add page to then Home screen, and confirm.
4. Getting the newest version

Changes to the app appear the next time you open or reload it. If something looks out of date, close the app and open it again (or pull down to refresh in the browser). You never need to add the icon again.

User guide

What the app shows

Every training and upgrade project from Ronnie's trackers (Free, Paid, Nucleus RMS, Nucleus QM, STING), combined in the Training Status master sheet. The app is read-only: nothing you do here changes any sheet.

The top bar shows when the data was last copied from the master sheet ("Synced from master sheet ..."). Updates happen automatically during the day.

Top bar and Menu

The dark bar at the top holds the Menu button, the sun / moon (Day / Night) button, the app name with the page you are on, and the logo.

  • Menu, Pages: Trainings, Completed, Trainers, Active PM Projects, Users (only you, the owner) and Help.
  • Menu, Day / Night: Day (light), Night (very dark grey with soft white text) or Automatic (follows your phone or computer). The sun / moon button switches between Day and Night. Your choice is saved with your account, so it follows you to every phone and computer, and is remembered on each device so even the sign-in screen opens that way.
  • Menu, User: your email and when the data was last synced, plus Set Up Face ID / Passkey, Change Password, Switch App (administrators with both apps only; goes straight to the Conference Tracker) and Sign Out.
Trainings page

Trainings are grouped by agency and state. Tap an agency to open or close it; Expand all and Collapse all do every agency at once. The line above the list shows how many trainings and agencies match your filters.

Filters

  • Date: In progress & upcoming (the default) hides finished items. Also: All dates, Past, This month, Next 30 days, Custom range (pick From and To), and No date set.
  • State, Category, Product, Status: pick one to narrow the list.
  • PM: one project manager's items. A training with two PMs (for example "Travis Pruitt, Ronn Brigman") shows under each of them.
  • Trainer: one trainer's items, or Unassigned for items with no trainer yet.
  • Search: any word in the agency, notes, product, status or portal number.
  • Sort and Order: list agencies alphabetically or by Date signed, Date assigned, Kickoff Meeting, Admin Training, User Training or Go Live. Items without that date go last, by start date. (These dates appear once they are added to the master sheet.)
  • Reset: puts the last thing you changed back to its default. Reset all: goes back to your default settings (all filters cleared, your default order, everyone's projects).
  • Show My Projects: only items where you are a trainer (your sign-in email must be on the master sheet's Trainers tab). Tap again for everyone. Admins see Select Trainer(s) instead: tick one or more trainers and tap Show. The choice applies on every page.

Your filters are remembered on this device, so the app opens the way you left it.

Reading a row

  • Date: start and end; a Now tag means it is happening today.
  • Category: colour-coded (Free, Paid, Nucleus RMS Upgrade, Nucleus QM Upgrade, STING Upgrade, Product, Other).
  • Trainer(s): Unassigned means no trainer is set yet.
  • PM: the project manager from the tracker.
  • Notes: tap a row to open its notes in a box beneath it, split by entry date (newest first unless you change it in My defaults). Text with no date shows last as Undated. The box also shows the tracker's agency name when it differs, the source and the last update. Tap the row again to close it.
  • Links / ID: Drive folder and portal project when the tracker has them.
Completed page

Trainings marked Complete or Closed (Closed = the agency never followed through). They no longer show on the Trainings page. The filters, Sort, Reset and Show My Projects work the same way; the default date choice shows all dates here.

My defaults

Menu, My defaults: your default list order and direction, and whether notes show the newest or oldest entry first. Saved with your account, so they follow you to any device. Reset all returns to these.

Card Reader

Menu → Card Reader (or the Card Reader tile when the app opens) holds the business cards of people you meet. Everyone with Training Status can add cards. Beside Card Reader in the Menu, N mine counts the cards you saved that you have not exported yet and N all every card you can see that you have not exported; each shows only when it is above zero.

The camera screen. Fill the white outline with the card and hold still.

When the card's edges line up and the picture is still, the outline turns thick green and the text reads "Hold still…". After one second the picture is taken by itself (or press Capture).

To read a card from a picture you already have, press Use a Saved Photo at the bottom of the camera screen; your photos open, and the app finds the card in the picture you pick. It is read and saved the same way as a camera picture.

If the live camera isn't available (or permission was turned down), the screen offers Take or Choose a Picture and Use a Saved Photo instead.

Active PM Projects page

Projects from Travis Pruitt's Current Projects Status Board, copied into the master sheet. One row per project with its state, status and project manager; tap a row for deliverables, date received, account manager, IT staff, trainers, hardware and notes.

  • Shows only active projects: anything Complete, Cancelled, Closed, Done, Not Included or On Hold is left out. The line above the list says how many were left out.
  • Filters: State, Status, PM and Search (agency, trainer, notes and more). Clear resets them.
  • "Board last updated" shows when the PM last changed that project on the board, so you can tell how fresh it is.
Trainers page

Each trainer's current assignments (not finished yet), busiest first. Tap a trainer to open or close their list (email, products and assignments); Expand all and Collapse all open or close every trainer. Tick Show inactive trainers to include trainers marked inactive. A trainer with a yellow note is named on a training but missing from the master sheet's Trainers tab; tell Ronnie.

Users page (Ronnie only)

Only the account owner sees Users in the Menu. Access to both apps is now managed on one Users page in the Conference Tracker: Users here opens a link to it. There, open the Menu, choose Admin, then Users. Tick Training Status to give someone this app; untick it to remove it. Unticking never deletes the account.

Something looks wrong
  • A training is missing or wrong: tell Ronnie. It is corrected in the tracker sheet and shows here after the next sync.
  • "Could not load data": check your connection and reload.
  • Face ID did not work: sign in with your password, then open the Menu and tap Set Up Face ID / Passkey again.
  • "Your account does not have access": ask Ronnie to add you.
